Statement

Growing up, I always loved doodling and scribbling but I never took it further than that. It wasn't until I started 3D modeling objects I wanted to build that I truly understood the importance of design and art. That realization led me to enroll in an art class at my local community college which I ended up really enjoying. This portfolio consists of some of the art pieces that I was most proud of as well as some personal drawings.
